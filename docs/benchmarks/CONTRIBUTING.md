# Share your results

**One simple table is enough.** Tell us your setup, the model you ran in SYNAPSE,
and the tokens/second you observed. You do not need a control run, comparison,
logs, hashes, repeated tests or a separate report.

## How to contribute

1. [Edit the benchmark README on GitHub](https://github.com/appatalks/Synapse/edit/main/docs/benchmarks/README.md).
2. Copy the [simple table template](TEMPLATE.md), fill in your results, and add
   it under a heading for your setup. If your setup already has a community
   table, you can add your model there instead. You do not need to use the
   control/hybrid comparison columns from the project's measured-results table.
3. Propose your changes and open a pull request. GitHub can guide you through
   creating a fork or branch if needed.

Quantization, execution mode, context size, settings, screenshots and comparison
results are **optional**. Include whatever you know; leave other details blank
or use `—`. A screenshot alone can help, but please put your reported speed in
the table so people can find it easily.

Community entries are self-reported results, not verified comparisons or quality
guarantees. Without a control, we simply list the reported rate rather than
calculate a speedup. Please leave out credentials and private information.
